# Watchtower

A media discovery and organizer app built for Android TV and Fire TV, designed to be used with a remote control.

Watchtower helps you keep track of what to watch: personalized recommendations, upcoming releases, your own lists and collections, and a clean player.

## Features

- **For You & Because You Watched** – recommendations based on what you actually watch
- **Coming to Watchtower** – upcoming movies and new seasons, with release dates right on the cards
- **Collections & Lists** – create your own, follow others, and keep them in sync with Letterboxd
- **Watch Party** – watch together with friends, in sync
- **Profiles** – every profile has its own watchlist, progress and settings
- **Skip Intro / Auto Skip Intro** – jump past intros, manually or automatically
- **Autoplay & Resume** – continue right where you left off
- **Built for the couch** – a clean, focus-driven interface made for a TV remote
